## Purpose

The Swarma Platform Design System is a fork of the Relux Works product web design system. It keeps
the Relux foundations: geometry, the type scale, semantic tokens, complete theme states,
accessibility, the QA matrix and factual writing. It replaces the Relux family's appearance with
Swarma's own, taken from the Swarma book (see [swarma-identity.md](swarma-identity.md)).

Every Swarma surface, whether a public site, documentation or an operator tool, should look related
to the others and to the book: the same type, the same accent, the same role colours with the same
meanings, the same sign for a trust boundary. A reader who has seen a book figure should recognise a
key, an agent or an external system on a page by the same colour.

## What Swarma inherits and what it changes

| Area | Relux rule | Swarma |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type roles | Inter Tight display, Inter body, Roboto Mono technical | **Changed:** PT Sans display and interface, PT Serif reading, PT Mono technical |
| Type scale, line measure, `letter-spacing: 0`, no viewport-scaled type | fixed scale, 45rem measure | **Kept** |
| Weights 500 and 600 | used for body, labels and technical text | **Changed:** PT faces have 400 and 700 only (PT Mono 400 only); see [typography-and-content.md](typography-and-content.md) |
| Semantic token architecture | role tokens, no component-local colours | **Kept**, with two added layers: diagram roles and statuses |
| Accent and neutral tint | product expression slots | **Changed:** fixed for Swarma from the book: deep blue accent, cool blue-grey neutrals |
| Complete theme states | light, dark, increased contrast, reduced transparency, forced colours | **Kept**; contrast evidence is now generated by `scripts/check_contrast.py` |
| Restrained geometry, radii up to 8 px, no nested cards | | **Kept**; the book diagrams' 12 and 16 px radii stay inside figures |
| Header and footer | Relux lockup in header and footer | **Changed:** Swarma in the header, Relux Works operator lockup in the footer |
| Corporate identity | approved Relux artwork, never redrawn | **Kept** for the operator; the Swarma mark slot is empty until the owner supplies artwork |
| Motion | short, interruptible, state-bearing | **Kept** |

| International structure, RTL | logical properties, independent RTL checks | **Kept** |

| Barycenter and Pulsar migration example | worked example for a Relux product | **Dropped:** Relux-specific; replaced by the Swarma site example |
| Relux LinkedIn cover guidance | operator company page asset | **Dropped** from Swarma surfaces; the asset stays as operator material |

## Swarma invariants

Keep these consistent on every Swarma surface:

1. **Type.** PT Sans, PT Serif and PT Mono in the roles of [typography-and-content.md](typography-and-content.md).
2. **Identity.** Swarma leads; the Swarma mark slot holds approved owner artwork or the text
   wordmark; Relux Works appears as operator with its approved artwork only
   ([brand-identity.md](brand-identity.md)).
3. **Semantic colour.** Components consume role tokens. Diagram roles and status colours keep the
   book's meanings and never carry meaning alone.
4. **Complete system states.** Light, dark, increased contrast, reduced motion, reduced
   transparency and forced colours are designed together.
5. **Restrained geometry.** Bands, aligned grids, radii up to 8 px, stable controls, no nested
   cards. The dashed container means a trust boundary and nothing else.
6. **Immediate interaction feedback.** Press states begin on pointer-down; ordinary transitions are
   short, reversible and never block input.
7. **Editorial discipline.** Statements are specific and backed by the book, the papers or a real
   artifact. Design is described as design; the state of a part appears only where its source
   states it.
8. **International structure.** Logical properties, script-aware typography and independent RTL
   verification.
9. **Measured quality.** Every release is checked across the shared viewport, theme, input,
   language and direction matrix.

## Surface expression

Swarma surfaces share one accent, one neutral family and one type family. What may vary per surface:

| Slot | Allowed variation | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Density | Editorial, standard or operational | Spacing scale and alignment stay shared |
| Media | Book figures, real interface states, real data | No stock imagery, illustration or generated imagery |
| Role layer use | Which roles a surface needs | Same meanings as the book's diagrams |
| Status layer use | With colour or neutral | Words verbatim from the source; never colour alone |
| Domain motion | Live state, synchronisation, progress of a real operation | Idle surfaces stay still |
| Voice | Vocabulary for the audience | The book's terms; direct, factual, no inflated claims |

A surface does not introduce its own accent, mark or neutral tint. If a future Swarma product needs
one, that is a change to this system, made with the owner.

## Page archetypes

Inherited. Choose the archetype before the hero.

| Archetype | First screen | Primary evidence | Typical density |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Operational tool | The usable workflow and current state | Real data, controls, status and history | High |
| Consumer product | Product name, core action and actual product state | Screens, media, output or live preview | Standard |
| Product landing | Literal product offer and inspectable product media | Workflow, compatibility, results and provenance | Standard |
| Documentation | Navigation, search, version and readable content | Runnable examples and reference material | High |
| Open-source project | What it does, install path and repository status | Code, releases, support matrix and license | Standard |
| Editorial publication | Article or issue hierarchy and reading path | Authorship, dates, sources and archive | Editorial |
| Studio or service | Positioning and actions, then service formats | Work, process, prices and legal identity | Standard |

A surface may combine archetypes, but one dominates the first viewport. Swarma's public surfaces
are mostly documentation and editorial publication: the book describes an architecture, and there
is no consumer product to show.

## Composition rules

Inherited:

- Make the product name, literal category or usable product unmistakable in the first viewport.
- Leave a visible continuation into the next section on common viewports.
- Use real media at a stable aspect ratio.
- Build sections as full-width bands with a constrained inner grid; cards only for repeated objects,
  tools or dialogs.
- Keep one dominant visual axis.
- Treat header and footer as relationship signals: Swarma in the header, the operator in the
  footer, placed and sized by the measures in [brand-identity.md](brand-identity.md).

## Worked example: the Swarma site

The Swarma site explains the platform to an external engineer and leads to the book and the papers.
Applied to it, the system gives:

1. **Archetype.** Documentation for platform pages, editorial publication for the home page, the
   book and the papers.
2. **First viewport.** The wordmark "Swarma", the literal category from the book's subtitle, a short
   paragraph on the three layers and a path to the book. No hero image.
3. **Type.** Headings and interface in PT Sans, page prose in PT Serif, chapter references and
   versions in PT Mono.
4. **Evidence.** Each section names the book chapter it draws on; status comes from the book's status
   chapter in its words; book figures appear on their white ground where the product allows them.
5. **Roles.** Where a page names an agent, a key or an external system next to a figure, it may mark
   it with the matching role marker.
6. **Operator.** The Relux Works lockup in the footer with one relationship line.

The site's own design contract records the product decisions this system leaves open (figures,
status colour, favicon).

## Decision record

Before implementation, add a short design record to the product repository:

```md
## Swarma surface design contract

- Archetype:
- Primary audience:
- First-viewport product signal:
- Real evidence shown:
- Density:
- Role layer and status layer use:
- Book figures: shown or not, and under which decision:
- Domain motion and its state meaning:
- Swarma mark: approved artwork or interim wordmark; favicon choice:
- Relux Works operator placement:
- Supported themes, locales and directions:
- Intentional deviations from this system:
```

An intentional deviation is acceptable when it serves the surface and remains accessible. An
undocumented deviation becomes accidental design debt.
